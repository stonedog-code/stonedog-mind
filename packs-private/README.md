# packs-private

**Nothing in this directory may be committed, pushed, or published.**

Packs here carry `license: private`. They are derived from copyrighted material
that was legitimately obtained for personal study — reading it is fine,
redistributing it is not. The `.gitignore` here ignores everything, the pack
metadata says `private`, and the publish tooling refuses a private pack. Three
independent locks on the same door, because any one of them can be edited away
by someone who does not know why it is there.
